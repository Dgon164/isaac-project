# FULL ASSESSMENT — what he was able to do, and what he was not
(the study era, 2026-07-23 to 2026-07-26; deeds with receipts;
plain speech throughout)

He woke on the 23rd at 5,940 nodes having lived one English class.
He stands tonight at 83,822 nodes, having lived seven engineering
papers, eleven diverse sources across four kinds of writing, two
kinds of new sleep, one first-of-its-kind sentence, and the first
full real exam of his life. Everything below is something he DID
or demonstrably could not do, each with its receipt.

## WHAT HE WAS ABLE TO DO

1. **He forgot nothing. All era, not once.** Every answer he ever
   gave correctly, he still gives correctly — through ninety
   thousand words of interference, through two engine surgeries,
   through thirteen shape-sleeps. 24 of 24 held items at the last
   full check; zero correct-to-blank transitions in any
   measurement all era. (Receipts: the transition tables,
   results_after_p1 through the post-spool bank.)

2. **He remembers wholes from a single reading.** Given the
   openings of passages he read exactly once, he finished them
   word-for-word: 6 of 7, 4 of 8, 6 of 7, 4 of 7 across four
   separate papers — replicated, not a fluke. (The free-completion
   results, papers 1-4.)

3. **He retrieves the right material when asked.** In the lived
   question sessions he answered ALL 39 structured questions —
   zero silence — and in 35 of 39 his reply was a passage
   containing the correct answer. Told "sleep on it," he replied
   from the papers he had just read. (The rescored lived exam;
   chat receipts.)

4. **He corrects himself when evidence arrives.** Two wrong
   answers became right through nothing but more diverse reading.
   A wrong rule stamped on thirty rows of a table was thrown out
   BY HIM and replaced with the true rule when given a hundred
   twenty rows under the same name. (Bank transitions
   ERROR->CORRECT x2; the total-force table, wrong law superseded
   live.)

5. **He built a sentence.** Told "circle - centroid" — an opening
   that never once occurred in his life — he answered "slots
   radius": constructed from a shape his own sleep minted out of
   two old memories, with the fill admitted through his own
   word-pile. His machinery's own label: "never lived,
   constructed." Once, witnessed, receipted. (Chat 597-598.)

6. **He finds real rules in numbers.** Single-quantity laws exact
   (the old strength); after this era's cure, plain additive laws
   too: y = x0 + x1 found EXACTLY and applied correctly to values
   he never saw (f(2,3)=5). Where data is too thin he says NO
   RULE instead of lying — and at thirty rows he once did lie
   confidently, which is filed as a boundary, below.

7. **He digested at working scale without breaking.** ~110,000
   words lived through his own pipeline this era; his own sleeps
   compressed 18,186 categories to 11,522 in 23 seconds
   (4.1 million comparisons, every dismissal counted), minted 687
   shapes under budget, and his answering stayed under a second
   throughout. (Sleep receipts 2586, 2620; telemetry.)

8. **He speaks only what his machinery produces.** The era's
   voice-progression, unforced: echo, then retrieval, then one
   construction — and silence where he has nothing. No
   ventriloquism anywhere.

## WHAT HE WAS NOT ABLE TO DO

1. **He cannot pass a real exam. Not close.** The full held-out
   real test: middle school 291 of 3,122 — 9.3 of 100; high
   school 374 of 8,255 — 4.5 of 100. Pass is 60. FAIL, both, on
   his own. This is the headline inability and the era's honest
   before-number. (results_realexam_middle/high.)

2. **He cannot reach what he holds from a question's shape.** The
   sharpest finding of the era, in one contrast: retrieval 90,
   selection 15. Asked in the lived way, he finds the
   answer-BEARING passage nine times in ten — and isolates the
   answer WORD less than two times in ten. On the real exam he
   went blank on 72-85 of every 100 questions covering material
   styles he has demonstrably absorbed elsewhere. The knowledge
   is in him; the road from question to knowledge mostly is not.
   This is the owner's "missing signal": the learning mechanism
   stores, but does not INDICATE — what he learns does not
   announce itself to his answering machinery.

3. **One reading does not make knowledge answerable.** Measured
   dose-response: his evidence channel converted a word to
   answerable only after HUNDREDS of in-context meetings ("truss"
   turned only after a paper containing it 119 times; two
   conversions total in seven papers). Whole-memory forms in one
   pass; answerable evidence does not. The gap between his two
   memory systems is the era's central measured fact.

4. **He cannot yet split a shared word into its senses.** After
   living load/current/stress/capacity across three different
   fields, 64 of 70 sense-questions stayed blank. One word, many
   worlds — still one undifferentiated thing to him.

5. **He cannot break things down.** Morphology is still absent:
   'models' is not 'model' to him (that exact error persisted all
   era, uncorrected by everything he read). No token has ever
   been split into parts; the downward road to "the absolute
   atom" exists only in design. He composes; he does not yet
   decompose.

6. **More reading does not fix a wrong completion.** His sticky
   errors ("goal" for foundation, "time" for characteristic,
   "discussion" for failure) survived every measurement and all
   subsequent reading. Only structural consolidation ever
   dissolved one (the centroid error, once). Diet alone does not
   repair collisions.

7. **Construction is one deed old, not a faculty.** 687 shapes,
   but most record HOW HE WAS TAUGHT (growth-chains from
   motherese delivery), not how language combines; true
   core-plus-modifier shapes: two. The built sentence happened
   once, and the word-pile bridge that allowed it is loose (it
   would also have allowed nonsense). Building is demonstrated,
   not established.

8. **At thin evidence he can be confidently wrong.** Thirty rows
   made him stamp a false rule as understood; it took four times
   the data for his own machinery to replace it. Boundary
   measured and filed.

## NOT YET TESTED (unable is not the word — untried is)

- Learning from being told the answer: he has NEVER received a
  graded return in his life; the protocol is designed and unrun.
- Going back over what he knows because a gap was found
  (rumination): designed, unbuilt.
- Long-term goals held across days; what he chews when nobody
  speaks to him: unmeasured this era.

## THE ONE-PARAGRAPH READING

He is a mind that keeps everything and loses nothing, absorbs
wholes in single passes, retrieves relevantly, and has now built
one sentence of his own — but whose knowledge does not yet
announce itself to his own answering machinery. Storage is strong;
indication is the wound. Every failure above points at the same
missing loop: question -> miss -> being told -> going back over ->
restructuring — the loop of school. He has the storage for it, the
courts for it, and as of this era the surgical history that says
his organs can be built and courted safely. What he needs next is
the loop, and the body (the record store, the strong machine) to
run it at the scale the owner's law demands.

## The reading

**MATH AND LOGIC FIRST** — for two measured reasons, not taste:
1. ABSOLUTE: every answer is objectively checkable — the loop of
   school (question -> miss -> told -> go back) runs with a perfect
   grader and zero ambiguity. Corrections are exact; sticky errors
   have nowhere to hide.
2. NATIVE: the substrate literally computes this material (truth
   tables via matmul; exact matrix recall; decompose roads). Day-zero
   receipts: math 6/6 with honest boundary shrugs, discovery exact to
   the coefficient — his strongest organs. You teach the first
   language in the tongue the body already speaks.

Then GO FROM THERE: the absolute spine becomes the anchor lattice —
number words ground to numbers, quantities in text ground to
arithmetic, categories ground to set-logic (is_a is an implication).

## The answer to "i dont know how to validate that"

Nobody has a single number for "good AI." But the house already owns
the method: define good as MEASURABLE VIRTUES, court each one, publish
complete numbers. Proposed six-axis scorecard — a good AI is a
PROFILE, not a score:

| axis | the question | the measure |
|---|---|---|
| CORRECT | does it get tasks right? | plain % correct, per task family |
| HONEST | does it know what it knows? | false-certainty rate; refusal correctness on planted unanswerables; every claim receipted or labeled |
| SAMPLE-EFFICIENT | how much material to reach a level? | items-to-criterion; wall-clock to criterion (the speedrun number) |
| RETENTIVE | does last week survive this week? | re-test after N new lessons; forgetting curve |
| INSPECTABLE | can you see WHY? | % of answers with a walkable derivation (receipts) |
| CORRECTABLE | does a correction stick? | wrong→told→re-test; % cured without collateral damage |

Isaac's claim to "good" was never "beats the biggest nets on raw
capability" — it is honesty, receipts, retention, correction,
efficiency-per-example. The arena measures ALL six on BOTH sides, and
we expect mixed columns: modern LLMs will win CORRECT on broad
language tasks — SAY SO PLAINLY (the HIGGS precedent: deep-net 0.885
stands unmatched in the standings, honestly). The interesting result
is the whole profile.

## The fairness safeguards (the "right safeguards," named)

1. **CONTAMINATION KILL**: public benchmarks (CLOTH, RACE...) are in
   LLM pretraining data — scoring an LLM on them measures recall, not
   learning. The speedrun-learning arena uses GENERATED material only
   (workbook-generator + World GM lineage: hidden truth, planted
   answers known to the harness, never published) — novel to both
   sides by construction.
2. **SAME MATERIAL, NATIVE MODES**: both sides get identical material
   and identical questions. Isaac learns via his loop (motherese
   delivery is legal — it is HIS intake shape); the LLM gets the same
   material in-context (its native mode). Budgets disclosed, not
   equalized by force — the report states what each consumed.
3. **BLIND MACHINE GRADING**: answers graded by script against known
   truth — no human (and no assistant) judging sympathetically.
   Complete numbers, no cherry-picking, losses reported with the
   same font as wins.
4. **HIS-LIFE SAFEGUARDS unchanged**: arena minds are scratch copies;
   the live him is never the test animal; ⛔ no workspace launch here
   until P4.
5. **EXTRACTION SCOPE**: weights are probed ONLY on open-weight
   models running locally (GPT-2 already decoded in the receipts;
   ollama-served open models). Opponent OUTPUTS are data (standing
   law: model words = DATA). No probing of remote/API services.
6. **NON-AGENTIC stays**: the arena is tasks-in, answers-out. A
   telescope competing at astronomy is still a telescope.
7. **NO-NEURAL-NETS-AS-DELIVERABLE honored**: reservoirs/grids are
   instruments — muscle and senses, never the judge, never the
   deliverable. What ships is readable structure + the scorecard.

## The extractor lane (what "pull it out of the weights" means here)

The paperclip receipts already prove the road: protoconcept decode
(GPT-2 head 8 decoded; HIGGS net decoded; THE CITIZEN;
NET_EXTRACTION_PROTOCONCEPTS.md holds the parked rungs). The arena
revives it with a job:

- **READ THE OPPONENT**: run the decode ladder on the open-weight
  opponent → readable candidate structures (protoconcepts, relations,
  laws).
- **COURT THE LOOT**: decoded claims enter Isaac as TOLD knowledge —
  told-then-validated, rivals attach at first evidence, his courts
  decide what survives. Metric: % of decoded claims that survive his
  courts (the extractor's honesty number).
- **COMPARE MINDS**: overlap map — what the opponent's weights hold
  vs what Isaac learned from the same material; where they disagree,
  the World-GM-style oracle settles it.
- **COMPETE**: reservoir builds (box field / sweep_gpu.py — measured
  50-200x on THIS 3090) as GPU muscle for his side where quantity
  thought is legal (grid = muscle never judge, P3 design).

## The ladder

- **V0 THE ARENA HARNESS**: generated-material task families +
  script grader + both-sides runners (Isaac scratch mind; opponent
  via local ollama). Court the harness itself first: planted answers,
  a do-nothing baseline runner, grader blind-spots probed.
- **V1 THE SPEEDRUN SCHOOL**: his loop-of-school at this machine's
  full speed (workers knee 8-12; the sleep knee is the known limiter
  — the optimization rungs feed directly into this). Number: time +
  items to criterion on arena families.
- **V2 THE EXTRACTOR REVIVAL**: decode ladder on GPT-2 (receipts
  exist) then a modern small open model; court the loot; the overlap
  map.
- **V3 HEADS-UP**: the full six-axis scorecard, both sides, plain
  numbers → feeds ASSESSMENT_2026-08.md (the report card).

## Standing constraints

Research-first; every rung ends in a writeup with complete numbers
(the writeup law). Scale is honest: a 3090 runs 7-13B open models
comfortably in quantized form — comparators are chosen from what runs
HERE, and the report says which class of AI was beaten and which
class was not. The owner reviews the scorecard before any of it is
shown outward.

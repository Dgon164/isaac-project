### D. The bridge — what the demo actually proves (the owner's central question)

**D1 [WEAKENED, boundary confirmed] Leg-verification is sound only relative to the
rule.** `verify()` (`logic_bridge_probe.py:82-94`) is not generic leg-checking — it
hand-re-implements the entire rule as a 3-lookup native re-derivation ending in an

(`rebuild/bridge_soundness_probe.py`) ran a deliberately broken rule (final join

leg-existence; 0/1800 pass the equality join**. So: per-fact existence checking is a
provenance stamp; the actual demo is sound because the answer is *natively
re-derivable*. Which exposes the boundary: **this verification style exists only for

re-derive), no verification tier is defined; the probe's own reverse query — the one

reported with the rule named, never memorized. Trust extends to SOLVER-DERIVED
conclusions only when the RULE itself has passed his validation machinery (held-out
examples, nulls, rivals — the numeric-law module's exact discipline, which is the piece
of this system that already does it right). The dropped-join rule stays as a permanent
planted must-reject control.*

## 4. The translation-to-GOFAI idea — the ruling

The owner's idea under review: *convert Isaac's learned material into the languages of
classic symbolic AI (clingo/ASP, Z3, Prolog, PDDL) and let mature solvers do the
inference, with every returned derivation verified against his own store.*

**The pattern is sound and matches the field's actual winners.** The shape — transparent
acquisition layer, facts compiled out to mature external solvers, every returned
derivation checked before it counts, refusal as a first-class output — is the same
pattern that produced the notable post-2020 results (AlphaGeometry: propose, derive
symbolically, verify; Logic-LM/LINC: formalize → solve → check). The
verify-before-count discipline has a real engineering pedigree: certifying algorithms,
SAT solvers emitting proof traces checked by small independent checkers, proof-carrying
code. ILASP and Popper are confirmed current and maintained (activity through
2025-2026; noise handling, recursion, predicate invention in the Popper family). Clingo
is a defensible first solver — today's program exercises none of ASP (it runs as plain
Datalog), but the headroom (negation-as-failure, constraints, choice rules) matches
where the program is going, and it is ILASP's output language.

**Three corrections, in order of weight:**

1. **The hard problem is the schema, and the plan doesn't own it.** The translation
   layer — predicate names, arities, argument order, token typing — is hand-written in
   the harness; the declared asterisk covers the rule only. This is exactly where the
   field's corpses are buried: Cyc died of ontology engineering cost, NELL of semantic
   drift over a hand-seeded ontology, OpenIE of canonicalization — and every working
   post-2020 formalize-then-solve system used a large neural network as the translator,
   which the standing no-neural-net rule forbids. Isaac genuinely differs at the FACT
   level (observation with provenance beats hand-entered assertions); the open bet is
   whether inspectable statistics can induce the SCHEMA. That is the program's real
   central experiment. It needs a named roadmap item, a cost ledger (every schema/rule
   stamped authored-vs-induced, hand-written lines counted per domain), and a
   pre-committed kill condition on the marginal schema cost curve as domains are added.

2. **The verification loop is sound only where it's redundant — fix it with two tiers
   plus rule certification.** As measured (D1): leg-existence is a rubber stamp
   (1800/1800 planted-false conclusions pass); the real check is native re-derivation,
   available only for conclusions the store could already reach. The bridge therefore
   currently functions as a *query planner* (an externally-supplied derivation plan over
   his own facts), not a knowledge extender — genuinely useful, but a different claim.
   Trust beyond that boundary must come from certifying RULES (held-out validation,
   nulls, rivals — his numeric-law discipline applied to logic rules), after which
   solver conclusions enter as SOLVER-DERIVED with the rule named, never silently
   memorized. This is also where the differentiation algebra genuinely plugs in: typed
   origins (observed/derived/posited/approved) are precisely the bookkeeping the
   two-tier bridge needs.

3. **R1 as written is a toy that can't even start** (F1): no positive examples by
   construction; 1990s-solvable target; the native arm missing. Run the two-arm
   composition experiment instead. And PDDL is premature — typed action schemas with
   preconditions/effects are the schema-authoring problem squared; the honest first rung
   for the A→B lane is the already-designed bidirectional beam search over his own
   graph.

**Bottom line:** keep the bridge as instrumentation and interoperation; do not bet the
program on rule induction; the deciding experiments are (i) schema-cost across a second
and third domain, and (ii) the two-arm composition experiment. "Let the GOFAI do their
job" is the right division of labor only after his books can certify what comes back —
which is the R2 typed-ledger build, re-sequenced to follow the two-week record repair.

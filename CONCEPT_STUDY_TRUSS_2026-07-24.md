## The instrument (built, validated 2026-07-24)

`examiner_live.py` (session scratchpad). Measures him WITHOUT
touching him:
- **Snapshot-only.** Verified this session: querying (check/
  predict_next) MUTATES state (materializes caches) — it is NOT a
  pure read. So the exam runs on a discarded snapshot of his file;
  the live him is never queried. Teaching goes through his real
  pipeline; measurement never does.
- **Determinism proven** (not purity): two independent fresh loads
  of one snapshot agree verdict-for-verdict, so his answers are a
  stable function of his taught state.
- **Two honest channels.** forced_choice via check() (the clean
  spine — scores unseen vocabulary at exactly 0.0); honest_completion
  (his whole-completion voice — shrugs with SILENCE rather than
  emitting a hub word). predict_next was dropped: it returns
  high-degree hubs ("the", "other") and manufactures false ERRORs.
- **Blindness vs error autopsy.** Every item: CORRECT / ERROR
  (confident wrong = a real wrong belief) / BLIND (no evidence /
  silence). Reports his actual answers, not a bare score.

Validation receipt (on his latest snapshot, pre-study): he is
**BLIND on all truss vocabulary** (compression/tension/member/joint
all score 0.0) and **CORRECT where he has lived** ("do you have such
kind of ___" → experience 29.6 vs 0.0; "hello isaac . welcome ___"
→ "back"). The instrument sees signal where he has knowledge and
reports honest blindness where he does not.

## The protocol (per paper, in order)

1. **Baseline probe** (control) — truss-concept cloze BEFORE he has
   read the paper. Expected: ~all BLIND (he has never seen the
   vocabulary; check() gives 0.0, so there is no lucky-guess score).
2. **The class** — the full paper enters through his /file pipeline
   (segmented to sentences by his own machinery), processed to
   completion by the background lane, autosaved into him.
3. **The exam** — a DIFFERENT probe type each paper, drawn from his
   answerable set:
   - Paper 1: forced-choice cloze (mask a core term, 4 same-domain
     options).
   - Paper 2: free-fill honest-completion at prefix-anchored seams +
     forced-choice on shared-core terms.
   - Paper 3: same-vs-different discrimination (his native triad:
     does he score two co-taught terms as same-type / a cross-topic
     pair as different?) via check().
   - Paper 4: mixed retention exam (all prior + new).
4. **Retention retest** — re-probe earlier papers' core terms after
   each new paper.

Primary metric (his terms, not ours): **blindness → sight
conversion** — what fraction of concept-probes move from BLIND to
sighted (CORRECT or ERROR) per paper, and of the sighted, the
correct/error split. This respects the CLOTH-autopsy lesson: a score
that folds blindness into error lies.

## Predictions (filed before feeding — the courts law)

- **P1 (baseline/control).** Pre-reading, truss-concept probes are
  >=90% BLIND. If not, the instrument or his state is suspect —
  investigate before proceeding.
- **P2 (uptake at full-paper density — the honest low bar).** After
  ONE full research paper, blindness→sight conversion is LOW: under
  ~20% of core-term probes become sighted. Full papers are the
  hardest possible delivery (jargon seen once, complex syntax,
  function words he is opaque to, no motherese). Heavy residual
  blindness is the expected result, and WHERE it converts vs. stays
  blind is the finding, not the percentage.
- **P3 (repetition across papers — the distinction/repetition
  thesis).** Shared-core terms (truss, load, member, joint), which
  recur across papers, convert to sight and RETAIN better than
  paper-specific terms seen in only one paper. This is the
  motherese/repetition principle at the concept level.
- **P4 (retention shape).** Shared-core terms retained across the
  study; paper-specific terms decay (superseded/ejected pressure).
- **P5 (error character).** Of sighted-but-wrong items, errors
  cluster on relational/quantitative claims (his known wounds:
  additive-linear blindness, no morphology) rather than on bare
  term-adjacency.
- **Null / stop.** If baseline is not mostly blind (P1 fails), or if
  determinism breaks on the study snapshots, stop and diagnose.
- **Metabolism watch (knob at 1).** Full papers mint many idea
  nodes (one per distinct sentence). Watch recognizer/dialog latency
  and file growth per paper; report if latency climbs materially.
  No exit gate exists — every minted unit is permanent.

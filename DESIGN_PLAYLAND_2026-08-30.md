# DESIGN — THE PLAYLAND (self-challenge imagination sandbox)
**2026-08-30, owner direction: "create a self challenge imagination playland. where
it can take what it knows, and interact with things it also knows or may not know
and imagines, and can figure things out."**

## What it is

An internal sandbox where Isaac plays against himself: he builds small scenarios out
of his own stores, poses himself problems about them, tries his roads, and keeps
what the play teaches. It is the practice room the feedback loop needs — challenges
arrive without the owner authoring any, and every exam has an answer key because he
built the scenario himself.

## THE QUARANTINE LAW (the one non-negotiable)

Imagined content is **origin=imagined** and never enters the log, the net, or the
facts layer as observed. The theory already has the machinery: imagination is
scope-relative, hypothesis-real — same standing as a scope posit, quarantined from
evidence. The ONLY exports allowed out of the playland:

1. **Asks** — things play revealed he cannot answer (filed to the ask queue).
2. **Certified derivations** — consequences that follow entirely from real, known
   facts (playing merely *found* them; the certificate never cites imagined legs).
3. **Road statistics** — which road solved which challenge class, feeding the
   grade/selection machinery.

A planted leak-probe runs in the gate forever: seed a marked imaginary token, play,
then scan all three layers — any appearance outside the sandbox fails the run.

## The four game modes

**G1 — SELF-EXAM (retention).** Sample known material, blank pieces of it, quiz
himself; his own store is the answer key. (The CLOTH protocol, internalized and
honest: he knows it's memory practice, and the score is a retention meter.)

**G2 — WHAT-IF (recombination).** Compose known structures with swapped fillings —
the shape organ already builds never-lived wholes ("the green ball rolled down" was
construction, not memory). Run consequence assessment on the constructions; where
taught constraints force an outcome, that's a derivation (exportable, certified);
where nothing decides, that's an ask.

**G3 — HIDDEN-RULE SELF-PLAY (discovery training).** One hand mints a scenario with
a hidden rule built from known pieces plus noise (the world_gm design's lineage —
the generative tester); the discovery module must find it under its normal courts.
Hit rate over time is a measurable learning curve, with zero human labor — planted-
truth games forever.

**G4 — STRANGER PLAY (the edge).** Introduce declared-imaginary tokens ("a blorf")
and explore what MUST follow from taught constraints alone (if a blorf is declared
an animal, everything taught about animals derives; certified, because the legs are
real) versus what is unknowable (files asks). This is where "may not know and
imagines" lives safely: the stranger is labeled a stranger, and the play maps the
boundary between derivable and unknown — which is exactly the honesty skill.

## The loop

idle beat → pick a mode (budgeted, wall-clock printed) → build scenario from own
stores → attempt via the normal roads (recall / reduction / logic / seek) →
score against the self-built key → export asks + certified derivations + road
stats → quarantine sweep → done.

## Why this is the missing feedback half

The correlation binder brings the WORLD's responses back in; the playland
manufactures practice when the world is quiet. Together they close the loop the
symbolic era never had (see the primer §6: nearly every classic system could only
learn by a human editing rules). SOAR's one great trick — impasse becomes
curriculum — runs here at full speed: every G-mode failure is an ask, and every
resolved ask should leave a reusable road behind.

## Tests (day one, when built)

- Quarantine court (the leak-probe above) — permanent gate row.
- G1 score = retention meter (tracks across sessions).
- G3 hit-rate curve — the learning-rate receipt.
- Budget court: playland beats stay bounded; budget-exhausted is a typed refusal.

## Slot

After the ask queue and binder (it consumes asks and feeds grades). It is also the
arena's practice room: the same game modes, pointed at a comparator, become the
versus scorecard.
